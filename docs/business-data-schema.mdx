# Business Data Schema

The business data schema below lists all available fields in a business
record. Note that actual business records (actually, most of them) may
not have every field present.

## Business Operation Fields

<ParamField body="hours" paramType="nested" showLocation="false">
The hours of operation for this business location.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="isClosed" paramType="boolean" showLocation={false}>
A true or false value for whether or not the business location is
closed.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="productsOrServices" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A list of products or services offered by this business.
</ParamField>

## Classification Fields

<ParamField body="name" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
The business location's name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="yearIncorporated" paramType="integer" showLocation={false}>
The year the business was incorporated.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="yearOpened" paramType="integer" showLocation={false}>
The year the business opened.
</ParamField>

## Contact Fields

<ParamField body="emails" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A list of emails belonging to the business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="facebookPageURL" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A URL pointing to the Facebook page for the business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="faxes" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A list of fax numbers for the business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phones" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
A list of phone numbers for the business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="twitter" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
The twitter username for the business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="websites" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
A list of websites for the business.
</ParamField>

## Firmographic Fields

<ParamField body="categories" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A list of category keywords used for this business across multiple
sources.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="claimed" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
A list of websites where the business location has been claimed by the
business' owner.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="numEmployeesMax" paramType="integer" showLocation={false}>
The upper bound on the number of people employed by this business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="numEmployeesMin" paramType="integer" showLocation={false}>
The lower bound on the number of people possibly employed by this
business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="people" paramType="nested" showLocation={false}>
A list of known people associated with or employed by this business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="primaryCategories" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A list of standardized categories to which this business belongs.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="revenueCurrency" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
The currency associated with the revenue fields.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="revenueMax" paramType="integer" showLocation={false}>
The maximum known value of the business' annual revenue.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="revenueMin" paramType="integer" showLocation={false}>
The minimum known value of the business' annual revenue.
</ParamField>

## Geographic Fields

<ParamField body="address" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
The physical street address for this business location.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="city" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
The city of this business location.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="country" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
The country code for the business location.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="geoLocation" paramType="geo_shape" showLocation={false}>
A location value storing latitude and longitude.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="latitude" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
Latitude coordinate.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="longitude" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
Longitude coordinate.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="neighborhoods" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
Neighborhoods where this business is located.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="postalCode" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
Postal or ZIP code.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="province" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
State or province.
</ParamField>

## Generic Fields

<ParamField body="dateAdded" paramType="date" showLocation={false}>
Date added to system.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="dateUpdated" paramType="date" showLocation={false}>
Last updated date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="descriptions" paramType="nested" showLocation={false}>
A list of descriptions for this business from various sources.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="features" paramType="nested" showLocation={false}>
A list of features associated with this business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="keys" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
Internal Datafiniti identifiers.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="languagesSpoken" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
Languages spoken at this business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="paymentTypes" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
Accepted payment types.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="sic" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
The SIC code for the business.
</ParamField>

## Hospitality Fields

<ParamField body="numRoom" paramType="integer" showLocation={false}>
The number of rooms available at this business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="rooms" paramType="nested" showLocation={false}>
A list of room types available at the business.
</ParamField>

## Restaurant Fields

<ParamField body="cuisines" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A list of cuisines offered by this business location.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="menuPageURL" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
A URL pointing to the menu for the business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="menus" paramType="nested" showLocation={false}>
A list of menu items offered by the business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="priceRangeCurrency" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
The currency associated with the price range fields.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="priceRangeMax" paramType="float" showLocation={false}>
The upper bound of the business' price range.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="priceRangeMin" paramType="float" showLocation={false}>
The lower bound of the business' price range.
</ParamField>

## Reviews Fields

<ParamField body="reviews" paramType="nested" showLocation={false}>
A list of reviews for this business.
</ParamField>

## SourceURL Fields

<ParamField body="domains" paramType="domain" showLocation={false}>
A list of each unique domain found in the sourceURLs field.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="imageURLs" paramType="text" showLocation={false}>
A list of image URLs associated with this business.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="sourceURLs" paramType="keyword" showLocation={false}>
A list of URLs used to generate data for this business location.
</ParamField>
