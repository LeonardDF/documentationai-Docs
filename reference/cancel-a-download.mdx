---
title: Cancel a Download
description: "Cancel an in-progress download. Cancelled downloads retain records added before cancellation."
---

<Callout kind="alert">

Cancel a download only while its status is `queued` or `running`. Downloads that have already completed cannot be cancelled. Cancelling stops the download but does not delete it: records added before cancellation are retained and remain retrievable through `GET /downloads/{id}`.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">

Result links for a download expire after 7 days. Retrieve the records before then.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">

Your account will still have credits deducted for the number of records that were added before the download was cancelled.

</Callout>
